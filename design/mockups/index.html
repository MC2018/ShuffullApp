Shuffull · App Redesign

Style drafts — round 2

You liked Aurora (01), Slate (04), and Velvet (05). Each now has its original plus three variations that take the same idea somewhere a little more intentional and less generic. Open any card — each is a self-contained mock of the four core screens (Home, Now Playing, Genre Jam, Library).

What changed across the variations. The duotone “rainbow” chrome and neon glow are pulled back in favor of curated, single-direction palettes — that was the main “AI-ey” tell. Slate gets real color (colored album art + a second accent) while staying dense and utilitarian. Velvet keeps the frosted-glass depth but each variation holds to one controlled palette instead of indigo+pink+aqua at once.

Aurora

album-art-forward · ambient color-bleed

The artwork tints each screen. The variations swap the living teal→violet accent for a single curated hue, flatten the glow, and calm the canvas.
